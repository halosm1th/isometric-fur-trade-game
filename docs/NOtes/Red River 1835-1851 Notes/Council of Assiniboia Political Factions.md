# Council of Assiniboia Political Factions 1844-1851
## Left - free traders, local peoples
- McDermont, free trader from Ireland
- Bunn, doctor from Ruperts land
- Grant, leader of Metis, free trader and sheriff
- Ross, Sherif, from England, kinda racist but had first nations children, author.
## Centre - religious center and others
- Joseph Norbert Provencher, The Rt. Rev. Bishop of Juliopolis
- William Cockran
## Centre Right - non-company men loyal to company
- James Bird
- John Pritchard
- Robert Logan
- John Peter pruden
- Macallum
## Right - company men, mostly from UK
- Court Recorded Thom
- Governors of the Colony
	- Cristie till 1849
	- Caldwell after 1849
