# Names Mentioned
## Council Names
The following names are mentioned as members of the Council of Assiniboia in the provided sources, from 1834 through the end of 1850. It is important to note that the sources do not always explicitly state the year someone was appointed to the council, but rather note the years when they were present at a council meeting.

- **George Simpson:** Governor of Rupert's Land, present at meetings in 1835, 1836, 1837, 1839 and 1844.

**Other Notable Individuals**

- **Louis Plouffe:** Hired as a jailor in 1838
- **Francis Goodschall Johnson:** Appointed Recorder of Rupert's Land in 1854, and attended Council of Assiniboia meetings starting in 1855

The list above includes individuals who attended meetings of the Council of Assiniboia. Some individuals may have been appointed to the council in a given year but not have attended any meetings in that period. The minutes of the meetings often contain other names of individuals who were not members of the council but were present for other reasons.
