# Music prompts for Gemini

Paste each prompt into Gemini (the same way the first six tracks were made).
Save each result as the file name given, as .mp3, in `assets/audio/music/`.
Then tell Claude, and it will add the new names to the season lists in
`Scripts/Core/Audio.cs` (`SeasonTracks`, `ReelTracks`, `MenuTracks`).

Shared style, for every prompt: instrumental, no vocals, 2-3 minutes,
acoustic period instruments only (fiddle, wooden flute, tin whistle,
bodhran, hand drum, cello, low strings, accordion is too late, so none),
recorded close and warm. It should sit under gameplay and not pull focus.

## Open water (summer at the post) - currently only 1 track of its own

1. `river_brigade_morning.mp3` (done) - A bright, steady paddling rhythm. A fiddle
   and wooden flute play a French-Canadian voyageur melody over a soft hand
   drum keeping a paddle-stroke beat. Hopeful, the boats are setting out.
2. `the_ship_in_the_roads.mp3` - Slow and expectant. Low strings and a solo
   fiddle; the annual supply ship has been sighted off Hudson's Bay. A sense
   of a big grey sea and a small wooden fort, quiet excitement building.
3. `goose_marsh_at_dawn.mp3` - Sparse and airy. Wooden flute over a held
   drone, with long pauses. Flat marshland, mist, birds rising. Calm.
4. `trading_at_the_gate.mp3` - Mid-tempo, a little playful. Fiddle and hand
   drum trading phrases, as if two sides are bargaining. Busy but friendly.

## Breakup (spring) - currently only 1 track of its own

5. `the_ice_goes_out.mp3` - Starts slow and cold with low cello, gradually
   warms as a fiddle melody comes in. Rivers cracking open after winter.
6. `first_canoes_inland.mp3` - Gentle, moving forward. Flute and plucked
   strings, a journey up a long river through spruce forest.

## Winter

7. `snowshoe_trail.mp3` - Slow walking pulse on a soft drum, a lonely tin
   whistle melody. Cold, white and wide, but not frightening.
8. `fire_in_the_long_night.mp3` - Intimate and warm. Solo fiddle playing a
   slow Scottish air, the sound of a small room with a stove, wind outside.

## Freeze-up (autumn)

9. `last_boats_before_the_ice.mp3` - Urgent but restrained. Low strings with
   a driving fiddle line; the last brigade racing home before the rivers
   freeze.

## Evenings in the men's house (dances)

10. `orkney_jig.mp3` - A fast, lively jig on solo fiddle with foot stomping
    and a bodhran. Rough and joyful, played by a working man, not a concert
    player.
11. `voyageur_chanson_instrumental.mp3` - A cheerful French-Canadian dance
    tune on fiddle with spoons and foot-tapping. Mid-tempo, singalong feel,
    no voices.

## Danger

12. `wolves_about_the_post.mp3` - Tense and low. Held cello notes, a slow
    drum, an uneasy high fiddle harmonic. For when animals or rivals threaten.
