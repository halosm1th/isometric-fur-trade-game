# Audio credits

Every clip in `assets/audio/` and where it came from. Licences were checked on each Freesound page on 2026-10-11. All the Freesound sounds used are **CC0** (public domain), so credit isn't required, but it's given here anyway. The raw downloads are in `assets/external/soundFX/` (git-ignored); the clips here were cut, trimmed, made mono where positional, resampled and loop-crossfaded from them.

## Synthesised stand-ins (2026-10-11)

Generated in code (numpy filters and oscillators) because nothing recorded was to hand. They are placeholders: replace any of them by dropping a recording in under the same name (more numbered takes are picked up automatically). No licence attached; they're original to this project.

| Clip(s) | What it imitates |
|---|---|
