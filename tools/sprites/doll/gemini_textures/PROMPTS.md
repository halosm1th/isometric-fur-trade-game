# Textures for the 3D voyageur (Project Zomboid manner)

In Zomboid the faces and clothes are pictures painted onto plain models.
These are those pictures. Save each result here, in this folder, under the
name given, as PNG if Gemini offers it. Square, 1024x1024 or 512x512.

General rule to add to every fabric prompt if Gemini adds shading:
"Flat, even lighting, no shadows, no highlights, no folds, viewed straight
down, no perspective. It will be wrapped onto a 3D model, so the light must
not be painted in."

## 1. The face - `face.png` (attach `face_guide.png`)

> This is a grey clay render of a man's head, straight on. Paint a face
> **directly over it, keeping every feature exactly where it is in the
> render**: the eyes in the eye sockets shown, the nose where the nose is,
> the mouth where the mouth is, the same size and position, the same
> 512x512 framing. Style: simple hand-painted low-poly game texture like
> Project Zomboid - flat colours, soft painted shading, no outlines, no
> photo detail. He is a French-Canadian voyageur of 1810, about 35: warm
> tanned skin (around #c99572), dark brown eyes, heavy dark eyebrows, a
> thick black moustache drooping past the corners of his mouth, a day's
> stubble, weathered cheeks. **No hair, no hat** - bald head, plain skin
> over the scalp and ears. Keep the plain grey background around the head.

If the features move, try again with: "The eyes must stay at the height
and width of the sockets in the clay render; do not move or resize
anything."
