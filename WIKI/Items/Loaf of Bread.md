## Description

Ship's bread and the Bay post's baking: a taste of home that the country cannot supply.

## Mechanics

- 25 nutrition for 1 lb. Sailors carry 3 each; labourers who start with it carry 2.

## Where to find it

- York Factory starts with 30; the ship lands 40 more. Not stocked by the Nor'Westers or inland posts.
