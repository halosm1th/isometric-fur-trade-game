## Description

A beaver taken from June to September: thin, patchy fur with little underhair, worth under half a prime pelt. The in-game name is 'Summer pelt'.

## Mechanics

- Worth 0.45 MB against 1 MB for a [[Beaver Pelt]]: trapping season is an economic decision.
- Spring returns brigade carry 10 of them from Oxford House to York.

## Where to find it

- Any unshot [[Beaver]] killed in months 6 to 9.
