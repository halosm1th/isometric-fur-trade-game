## Description

Webbed snowshoes: without them the winter country is a wade.

## Mechanics

- Worn like clothing (toggle). Worn, snow is no trouble and you move at full pace; the journal reads 'snow at full pace'.

## Where to find it

- York starts with 4, Oxford House 2; one can be drawn from the York storeroom per person; players of the Bushwise background carry a pair, and band-born players start with them.
