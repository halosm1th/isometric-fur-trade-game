## Description

The Company's famous striped blanket, graded by 'points'. A staple of the Standard of Trade and the most valuable ordinary garment.

## Mechanics

- Warmth 5, worth 4 MB: the dearest cloth good in the code (a musket is 12 MB).
- Withheld from the lowest ranks: a store chest releases one only to men with standing 10 or better.
- The autumn outfit to Oxford House carries 6 (the dev test brigade also carries them).

## Where to find it

- York starts with 12; the ship lands 20 a year.
