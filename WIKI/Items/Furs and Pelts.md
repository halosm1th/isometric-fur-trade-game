# Furs and Pelts

**Status:** Implemented

Raw skins. Beaver is the engine of the trade; the other furs trade at lower values. Pelt quality is seasonal (a summer beaver is thin) and a musket ball tears a skin. See [[Fur Prices]].

| Item | Weight (lb) | Worth (MB) | Effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Beaver Pelt]] | 1.5 | 1 | - |
| [[Caribou Hide]] | 6 | 0.5 | - |
| [[Holed Hide]] | 6 | 0.2 | - |
| [[Shot-through Pelt]] | 1.5 | 0.35 | - |
| [[Summer Pelt]] | 1.2 | 0.45 | - |
| [[Wolf Pelt]] | 3 | 0.6 | - |

## Notes

- Prime beaver = 1 MB, summer beaver 0.45, shot-through 0.35.
- Most of a post's returns by value are beaver; at York 22 prime beaver sit in the store at the start.
- Design doc 13.6's formal 'quality rating' per pack is planned; in this build quality is expressed through different item ids.
