## Description

A grey wolf pelt: a prized trophy and a small trade item.

## Mechanics

- Worth 0.6 MB, 3 lb. A Factor with low loyalty (below 0.35) and ambition (above 0.5) skims 10 percent of the beaver and wolf pelts out of the ship's hold, a record the Factor's books will not show.

## Where to find it

- An unshot [[Wolf]]. The Assiniboine start with 3; the spring returns carry 10.
