## Description

The hooded blanket coat of the voyageur and the labourer, cut from a blanket and belted with a sash.

## Mechanics

- Warmth 4.5. Worn clothing stacks, so a capote under a robe is warmer than either.
- The default starting coat for HBC and NWC hands (labourers and voyageurs start in one; sailors carry one).

## Where to find it

- The ship lands 6 a year; new hands start with one.
