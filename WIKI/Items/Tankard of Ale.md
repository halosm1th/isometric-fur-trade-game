## Description

The Company's small beer, a little nourishing and a little warming. Ale is a small meal and a warm glow.

## Mechanics

- Quenches 45 thirst, feeds 6, warms by 4 and adds 12 intoxication.
- Much milder than rum (35 intoxication per dram); see [[Dram of Rum]].

## Where to find it

- York Factory starts with 20, the ship lands 30 more per year, the Nor'Westers start with 30.
