## Description

Dried buffalo meat pounded with melted fat and berries, packed into skin bags: the portable food of the whole fur trade. One game piece is a day-ration slab (a real bag weighed about 90 lb). The Assiniboine and the plains bands make it; the posts cannot do without it.

## Mechanics

- The densest everyday food in the game: 45 nutrition for 1.5 lb.
- It keeps: unlike fresh meat it does not spoil in a cache.
- Raiders go for it first. The loot order in [[Rivalry]] is pemmican, dried goose, dried meat, rum, trade goods, beaver pelts, bread, musket.
- A post buys it from bands at two pieces of pemmican for one piece of trade goods (see [[Barter Rates]]).

## Where to find it

- Made weekly by the wintering [[Assiniboine]] cache (+14 pieces a week while the camp is on its winter ground), and sold on to whichever post will take it when the cache holds more than 30 pieces or 3 per mouth.
- York starts with 60; the ship lands 30 a year; the Nor'Westers start with 40 and their July brigade lands one third of the year's outfit pieces as pemmican.
- Traded north from the Missouri towns: the Assiniboine hand over one pemmican for every three of corn they carry up ([[Indian Corn]]).
