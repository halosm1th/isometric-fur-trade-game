## Description

Saskatoons, cranberries and rosehips in their season. Not much of a meal, but they are what actually answered the scurvy in this country.

## Mechanics

- 10 nutrition, 0.6 lb. Eating one resets the days-since-fresh-food counter, which is what cures or prevents scurvy.
- Fresh food: it goes bad in a post or camp cache at 12 percent a day (5 percent while the country is frozen), and eating it resets the days-since-fresh-food clock that drives scurvy (see [[Health, Wounds and Illness]]).
- Berry season runs July, August and September. Every camp and post gathers (a camp adds at least 2 or 4 per mouth, the York bands 3 per mouth, a post 2 per mouth each week).

## Where to find it

- Gathered in season by camps and posts (see [[Gathering and Gardens]]).
- The farming Missouri towns add 3 per mouth in July and August.
