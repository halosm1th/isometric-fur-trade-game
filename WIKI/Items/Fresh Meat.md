## Description

Venison, buffalo, beaver tail: whatever the hunt brought in today. The in-game item is called 'Fresh meat'.

## Mechanics

- 30 nutrition for 2 lb.
- Fresh food: it goes bad in a post or camp cache at 12 percent a day (5 percent while the country is frozen), and eating it resets the days-since-fresh-food clock that drives scurvy (see [[Health, Wounds and Illness]]).
- The yield of a kill: buffalo 70, caribou 18, beaver 3.

## Where to find it

- Hunting [[Buffalo]], [[Caribou]] and [[Beaver]] (see [[Hunting]]).
- The Cree cache starts with 30; wider-country bands add 2 per mouth each week.
