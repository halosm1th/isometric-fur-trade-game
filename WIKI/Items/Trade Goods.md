# Trade Goods

**Status:** Implemented

The manufactured goods the trade was built on. Their worth in Made Beaver is what a counter or a camp counts them at. 'Trade goods' is also the name of the general-purpose piece, which is the coin at most counters (see [[Trade Goods (item)]]).

## Notes

- The named small goods (tobacco, beads, awl, stroud, powder, token) were added 2026-10-11 from the HBC Standard of Trade.
- Manufactured goods are deliberately not craftable (design doc 12.6): nobody in Rupert's Land can make them. Planned, not enforced by a crafting system yet.
