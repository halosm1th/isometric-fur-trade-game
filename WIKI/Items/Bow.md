## Description

A hunting bow, still the weapon of most of the country's hunters.

## Mechanics

- Damage 30, range 260, 1.6 s cooldown (about 19 damage per second): faster than a musket and silent. NPC trappers prefer it for beaver and wolf so they do not ruin the pelt.

## Where to find it

- A hunter NPC in the York scene starts with one; one player background and the Metis or band elders in the character sheet carry one.
