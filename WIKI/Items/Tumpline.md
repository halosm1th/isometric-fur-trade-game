## Description

The leather strap over the forehead that let a voyageur carry two ninety-pound pieces over a portage.

## Mechanics

- Adds 40 lb to what you can carry before slowing. Only one of each pack counts.
- Starting gear for a labourer; Voyageur characters start with one.

## Where to find it

- York starts with 4, NWC 6, drawable from the York storeroom by anyone (one per person).
