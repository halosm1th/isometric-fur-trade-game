## Description

The harvest of the Mandan, Hidatsa and Arikara gardens on the Missouri, and the staple of the plains trade. It is food and trade stock, not physic.

## Mechanics

- 32 nutrition for 1 lb; it keeps.
- Not counted as fresh food, so it does not cure scurvy.

## Where to find it

- Each Missouri farming town adds 30 corn per mouth in September and October.
- Through June to September nomadic camps within about 26,000 px of a town swap dried meat for corn at 2 corn per piece of meat.
- The [[Assiniboine]] carry 40 up to the Bay country and hand over one pemmican for each three (see [[Missouri Villages]]).
