## Description

Whitefish and pike from the nets and the ice-holes. A line in the open water lands one or two fish in about 45 percent of casts.

## Mechanics

- 22 nutrition for 1 lb.
- Fresh food: it goes bad in a post or camp cache at 12 percent a day (5 percent while the country is frozen), and eating it resets the days-since-fresh-food clock that drives scurvy (see [[Health, Wounds and Illness]]).
- A fishing net doubles-plus-one a fishery's daily take (see [[Fishing Net]]); an ice chisel keeps the hole open.

## Where to find it

- [[Fishing]] with a [[Fishing Line]], a [[Fishing Net]] or through the ice.
- Brigade canoes add 1 plus a third of their crew in fish each day on the water.
