## Description

The prime beaver pelt is the unit the whole trade is counted in: one pelt is one Made Beaver (MB).

## Mechanics

- Worth exactly 1 MB, and every price in the game is relative to it ([[Made Beaver]]).
- Prime beaver comes from trapping in autumn, winter and spring. From June to September the same animal gives only a [[Summer Pelt]], worth 0.45 MB.
- Shot with a musket the pelt is torn and becomes a [[Shot-through Pelt]] (0.35 MB).
- Posts pay for it at PeltPrice (goods per MB): York 1.0, Oxford House 1.0, wider-country posts 1.0, Nor'Westers 1.3; see [[Fur Prices]].
- Bands also take beaver as the coin they pay in when they buy from you.

## Where to find it

- [[Trapping]] with steel traps at a [[Beaver]] lodge (catch chance per trap per night 7 percent at a full lodge, scaled by lodge density and the setter's skill).
- Bought off bands at their camp.
- Starting caches: York 22, Nor'Westers 10, Oxford House 8, Cree 6, Assiniboine 4, wider posts 4.
