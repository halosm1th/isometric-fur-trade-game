## Description

A post's winter came in casks: eight birds salted down in brine. Nobody eats one at a sitting; it is a store, not a meal.

## Mechanics

- 240 nutrition per cask (about 7 person-days of food at the game's 33.6 a day), but 60 lb to move.
- When a household has no loose food on the shelf, the first cask is broken open into 8 dried geese.
- Brigade manifests carry them as provisions: the autumn outfit to Oxford House includes 6 casks, and a canoe crew short of food opens a cask into 7 dried geese.

## Where to find it

- Packed from the goose flight (the size of the goose hunt target scales to the post's mouths).
- Included in the autumn brigade manifest sent from [[York Factory]] to [[Oxford House]].
