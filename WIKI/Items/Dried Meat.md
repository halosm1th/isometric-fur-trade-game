## Description

Thin strips of meat dried over the fire: the band's winter store, and the thing a post buys when the shelves run low.

## Mechanics

- 35 nutrition for 0.8 lb: the best nutrition per pound of any ordinary food after pemmican.
- Keeps in a cache.

## Where to find it

- Wider-country posts and camps add it weekly (a post 5 to 7 per mouth, a band 4 to 8 per mouth).
- Posts buy it and pemmican from bands with trade goods; Oxford House spends up to 10 goods a week for 20 pemmican and 10 dried meat when it is short.
