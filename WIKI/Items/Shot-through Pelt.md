## Description

A ball tears a pelt, and the graders in London pay a fraction.

## Mechanics

- Worth 0.35 MB; the beaver or wolf is the same animal, but a musket kill spoils the fur. Use a trap, bow or knife for furs.
- Applies to beaver and wolf kills made with a musket.

## Where to find it

- A [[Beaver]] or [[Wolf]] killed with a musket.
