## Description

The copper kettle is the most prized of all trade tools: a pot over the fire, the thing that made brewing, boiling and melting snow easy.

## Mechanics

- Using one calls the drink action. A post needs a kettle in its stores to brew [[Spruce Beer]].
- Worth 2 MB.

## Where to find it

- York starts with 3 and the ship lands 4; Cree cache 2, NWC 2, Oxford House 1, Assiniboine 1; new hands carry one.
