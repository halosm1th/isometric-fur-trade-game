# Clothing

**Status:** Implemented

Garments that add warmth. Everything worn adds together; wearing a capote under a robe stacks 4.5 and 8. A worn garment can be taken off and stowed by using it from the hotbar. Snowshoes are treated as 'worn' gear but are listed under [[Tools]].

| Item | Weight (lb) | Worth (MB) | Effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Buffalo Robe]] | 8 | 2 | Worn: warmth 8 (layers stack) |
| [[Capote]] | 4 | 3 | Worn: warmth 4.5 (layers stack) |
| [[Hide Coat]] | 5 | 2 | Worn: warmth 7 (layers stack) |
| [[Point Blanket]] | 5 | 4 | Worn: warmth 5 (layers stack) |

## Notes

- Warmth ranking: Buffalo Robe 8, Hide Coat 7, Point Blanket 5, Capote 4.5.
- Warmth matters in the Exposure need; see [[Needs and Survival]] and [[Fire and Warmth]].
