# Tools

**Status:** Implemented

Tools, containers, fuel and the kit of survival. Packs add carrying capacity; traps, lines and nets feed the household; firewood is the fuel of every hearth. Firewood is an ItemKind.Fuel item rather than a Tool, but it lives here.

## Notes

- Tumpline and pack basket add 40 and 30 lb of capacity; they do not weigh against you to carry.
- Steel traps are used at beaver lodges; see [[Trapping]].
- Snowshoes are worn like clothing.
