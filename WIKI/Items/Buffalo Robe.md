## Description

A winter buffalo hide dressed with the hair on: a bed and a coat at once, and eight pounds to carry. Warmth first, trade good second. Its icon is borrowed from the caribou hide (placeholder).

## Mechanics

- Highest warmth rating of any garment: 8 (hide coat 7, point blanket 5, capote 4.5). Worn layers add together.
- Worth 2 MB.

## Where to find it

- Dropped by an unshot [[Buffalo]] (a musket kill drops a Holed Hide instead, see [[Holed Hide]]).
