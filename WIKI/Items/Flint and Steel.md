## Description

A striker and a piece of flint: the fire-starter.

## Mechanics

- Lights a cold hearth with one piece of firewood, or makes a camp fire on open ground (needs firewood; not indoors).
- Crouched (Ctrl) against a flammable building it starts a fire on purpose. The residents will come for you (rapport -60).

## Where to find it

- New hands start with one; band players too.
