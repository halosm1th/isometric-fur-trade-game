## Description

A skin or barrel of clean water. Water is not scarce here; the cost is access, and in winter it is paid in warmth.

## Mechanics

- Quenches 50 thirst. Has no trade value (0 MB).
- Drinking from a river or lake costs nothing in summer. Frozen, it costs 8 warmth; at a lit fire you can melt snow instead for a little fuel (0.3 hours).

## Where to find it

- Every household starts with a barrel (24 at York, 10 to 16 elsewhere); the new hand carries 2.
