# Food and Drink

**Status:** Implemented

Everything edible or drinkable. Each food has a nutrition value added to the Hunger need; a person burns about 33.6 nutrition a day (1.4 an hour) in the household food-days sum. Fresh foods spoil at 12 percent a day in a cache (5 percent frozen) but cure scurvy; dried and salted foods keep but do not.

## Notes

- Cure scurvy: Fresh Meat, Goose, Fish, Hare, Ptarmigan, Berries, Spruce Beer (eating or drinking one resets the days-since-fresh counter).
- Intoxicating: Ale (12) and Rum (35).
- Rationing order for a household draws berries, hare, ptarmigan, fish, venison first and pemmican last (goose moved to last in autumn).
