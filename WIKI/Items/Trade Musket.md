## Description

The Northwest gun or 'trade musket', the most expensive thing in the Standard of Trade. In-game it is called 'Trade musket'.

## Mechanics

- Damage 55, range 340, 4 s cooldown (about 14 damage per second): the hardest hit and the slowest loader.
- Brings a 'shoot' animation; NPCs with one are counted as fighters.
- Kills with it ruin hides: furs become [[Shot-through Pelt]] or [[Holed Hide]].
- Selling a musket to a band at odds with its neighbours raises their standing with the Company by 6 and is remembered; a band at war asks about guns first and pays 30 percent over price for what it lacks. The player may refuse to put muskets on the table, which costs 5 standing and rapport -6.
- Only men the Factor trusts (standing 15) can draw one from the York storeroom.

## Where to find it

- York starts with 6, the ship lands 8 a year, the NWC brigade lands 4, Oxford House holds 2; sailors on the ship carry them.
