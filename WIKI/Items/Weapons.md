# Weapons

**Status:** Implemented

Four weapons exist in the code: the trade musket, bow, knife and tomahawk. Damage is per hit. The best weapon carried by raw damage is the one NPCs draw by default; the player picks one from the hotbar. See [[Combat]] for the full fight model.

| Item | Weight (lb) | Worth (MB) | Effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Bow]] | 2 | 2 | Damage 30, reach 260, cooldown 1.6s (about 18.8 dps) |
| [[Knife]] | 0.5 | 0.5 | Damage 18, reach 60, cooldown 0.7s (about 25.7 dps) |
| [[Tomahawk]] | 2 | 1.5 | Damage 28, reach 70, cooldown 1s (about 28.0 dps) |
| [[Trade Musket]] | 9 | 12 | Damage 55, reach 340, cooldown 4s (about 13.8 dps) |

## Notes

- The musket hits hardest but takes 4 seconds to reload and ruins pelts.
- The tomahawk and knife are the fastest at close range; reach is only 60 to 70.
- The bow is the hunter's weapon for fur: a clean kill leaves a prime pelt.
