## Description

Goose split, salted and smoked or frozen, the staple of a Bay post's winter table. The spring and autumn goose flights fill the stores with it.

## Mechanics

- 30 nutrition for 1 lb. A good trade-off of weight and food.
- It keeps in the cache (it is not counted as fresh food, so it does not stop scurvy).
- Opening a [[Cask of Salt Geese]] when the shelves are empty yields 8 dried geese.

## Where to find it

- Largest single stock at [[York Factory]] at the start (120). The ship lands 40 more; the Nor'Westers and Oxford House start with 40 and 30; the Cree cache with 20.
- Made from the autumn goose hunt (see [[Goose (item)]]).
