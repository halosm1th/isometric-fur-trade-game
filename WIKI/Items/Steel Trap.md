## Description

The Company's steel trap, the single most important tool in a trapper's pack.

## Mechanics

- Used at a [[Beaver]] lodge to set a line (up to 6 at once). The catch waits in the traps for the owner to check; others can poach it.
- Worth 1.5 MB, 4 lb.

## Where to find it

- York starts with 10, the ship lands 12, the NWC brigade lands 8 every July, Oxford House starts with 8, the Cree cache with 8; new hands carry 3.
