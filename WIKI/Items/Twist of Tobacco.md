## Description

A twist of Brazil tobacco, one of the staples of the Standard of Trade. A pipe is smoked before every trade.

## Mechanics

- Worth 1 MB, 1 lb.
- Offered as an opening gift it counts as a pipe: margin +0.12 (others +0.05), patience +0.4 (others +0.25) and rapport +8 (others +4), plus its value; see [[Trading and Barter]].
- The gift menu lists tobacco first.

## Where to find it

- York starts with 30, NWC 25, wider posts 8.
