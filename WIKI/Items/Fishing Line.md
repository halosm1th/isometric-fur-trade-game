## Description

A hand line and hooks.

## Mechanics

- Used near open water: a 3-second cooldown and a 45 percent chance to land 1 or 2 fish. Frozen water needs jigging through the ice.
- A household with a line (or a net) can send people fishing as a daily task.

## Where to find it

- Stocked everywhere (York 6, NWC 5, Cree 4, Oxford 3, posts 2); players start with one.
