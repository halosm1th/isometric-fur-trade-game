## Description

A felling axe, for the endless demand for firewood.

## Mechanics

- Use it within 150 px of a tree to fell and split a spruce: 4 firewood (6 at Woodcraft 0.5+), a 1.2 s action (0.8 s at 0.5+). Practice raises Woodcraft by 0.02.

## Where to find it

- York starts with 4, Oxford 2, wider posts 1; new hands carry one.
