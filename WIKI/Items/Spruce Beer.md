## Description

Brewed at the post all winter from spruce boughs: the Company's own remedy for scurvy when there were no berries.

## Mechanics

- Quenches 40 thirst, feeds 4 and warms by 3. No intoxication.
- Counts as fresh food for scurvy: drinking it resets the days-since-fresh-food clock.
- Out of berry season a post with a kettle and firewood brews 3 per man who has gone more than 14 days without fresh food, costing 2 firewood per brew.

## Where to find it

- Brewed by a post when its men have been too long without anything fresh (see [[Gathering and Gardens]]). Not stocked at the start.
