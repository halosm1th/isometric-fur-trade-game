## Description

A freshly killed goose. Not to be confused with the animal on the [[Goose]] page.

## Mechanics

- 35 nutrition for 1.5 lb.
- Fresh food: it goes bad in a post or camp cache at 12 percent a day (5 percent while the country is frozen), and eating it resets the days-since-fresh-food clock that drives scurvy (see [[Health, Wounds and Illness]]).
- In the autumn goose season the rationing order puts dried food ahead of fresh goose so the flight is saved for salting.

## Where to find it

- Killed from the goose flights (see [[Hunting]]).
