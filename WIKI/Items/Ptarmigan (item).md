## Description

A willow ptarmigan, the winter bird of the barrens. It has no icon of its own yet and borrows the bird's own death frame (placeholder art).

## Mechanics

- 14 nutrition for 0.8 lb.
- Fresh food: it goes bad in a post or camp cache at 12 percent a day (5 percent while the country is frozen), and eating it resets the days-since-fresh-food clock that drives scurvy (see [[Health, Wounds and Illness]]).

## Where to find it

- Killed from the [[Ptarmigan]] flocks; NPC hunters bring them in as game.
