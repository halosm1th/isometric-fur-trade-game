## Description

Split spruce. Everyone in the country lives off it; a hearth burns about three hours on one piece.

## Mechanics

- Burns 3 hours on a lit hearth when fed (see [[Fire and Warmth]]).
- A flint-and-steel strike with one piece lights a cold hearth or makes a camp fire in the open.
- Felling a spruce with an [[Axe]] yields 4 firewood, or 6 once Woodcraft is 0.5 or better.
- Has no trade value (0 MB) but is the heaviest common item at 5 lb.

## Where to find it

- Chopped with an Axe; every household stocks it (40 at York, 20 to 30 elsewhere), and wider-country posts top up 6 when they fall below 20.
