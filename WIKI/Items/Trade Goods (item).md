## Description

The general 'piece' of the Standard of Trade: assorted cloth, kettles, knives and trinkets counted as one unit of goods. It is the coin at every counter.

## Mechanics

- Worth 1 MB, 3 lb. At a company counter pelts are paid for in trade goods at the post's price (see [[Fur Prices]]).
- When the player buys from a counter, the price is counted in trade goods.
- A clerk or postmaster is paid 6 or 12 pieces when the ship sails if rated that high.
- The post is 'bought out' when it cannot cover the payment; the sale stops there.
- The 'single TradeGoods item' predates the named small goods ([[Twist of Tobacco]], [[Bunch of Beads]], [[Awl]], [[Yard of Stroud]]) that were added on 2026-10-11.

## Where to find it

- York starts with 90, the ship lands 120 a year, NWC 70 (plus 60, 90 or 130 pieces each July), Oxford House 50, wider posts 40.
