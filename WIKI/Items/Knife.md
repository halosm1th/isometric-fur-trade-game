## Description

A belt knife, carried by nearly everyone.

## Mechanics

- Damage 18, reach 60, 0.7 s cooldown (about 26 damage per second): the best sustained damage at close range, the weakest per blow.

## Where to find it

- Labourers, voyageurs and sailors start with one; band-born players too.
