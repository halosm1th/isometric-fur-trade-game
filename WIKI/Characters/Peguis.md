## Who they were

A Saulteaux chief at Red River who helped the Selkirk settlers survive and later signed the 1817 treaty with Selkirk.

## In the game

The content list calls him an "ally of the settlers". The Plains Ojibwa (Saulteaux) are a band in the atlas with their own chief Ajijaak, but Peguis is not that character ([[Ojibwe]]).
