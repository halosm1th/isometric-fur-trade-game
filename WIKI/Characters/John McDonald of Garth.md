## Who they were

A Highland NWC partner who served in the Athabasca and Red River countries and later retired to Garth in Scotland.

## In the game

`Council.Seed()` makes him the Red River councillor with two shares. His mid-range suspicion and loyalty make him a swing voice at the July council ([[Councils and Accords]]).
