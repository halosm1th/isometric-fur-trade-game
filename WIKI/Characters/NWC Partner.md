## Role in the world

The partner is the NWC's equivalent of the [[Factor]]: he gives the day's orders at the wintering post and sets the price "usually a shade better than the Company's" within the mandate of the July council ([[NWC Ledger]], [[Councils and Accords]]). He has a stock of 70 trade goods, 40 rum and 40 pemmican at the house at the start, and a quota of 80.

Unlike the Factor, he does not command a hierarchy but a partnership: the directive for the year (price, outfit of 90 pieces, rum share, whether the Bay is to be pressed) is the output of the council he attended, or the one he sent letters to.

## Behaviour

Morning: he hands out tasks and then wanders his work cell. His men are voyageurs and the odd clerk. With tensions high (70 or more) and raids sanctioned, he may send armed men across the river for the Bay's stores, and with a rival's raid on his stores he orders guards.

## Player route

An NWC player's prologue ends with the order to report to the partner. His rumour: "the partners at Fort William want that band turned from the Bay men this year" ([[NWC Path]]).
