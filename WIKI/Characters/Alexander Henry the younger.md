## Who they were

An NWC partner and journalist who wintered at Pembina and in the Saskatchewan and Columbia districts. His journals are the best record of Red River posts. He drowned in the Columbia in 1814.

## In the game

`Council.Seed()` includes him as "the Saskatchewan" with the least suspicious profile of the six councillors (0.3). `docs/world-content-needed.md` points to his journal as a source for posts.
