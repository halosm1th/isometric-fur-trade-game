## Who they were

Thomas Douglas, Earl of Selkirk, a Scottish peer who began buying HBC shares in 1808. His brother-in-law Wedderburn joined the Committee in January 1810, and in May 1811 the Company granted him 116,000 square miles on the Red River in return for his supplying 200 men a year. He settled Highlanders and Irish at the Forks, backed Governors [[Miles Macdonell]] and [[Robert Semple]], and after Seven Oaks led a force of de Meuron mercenary soldiers to take Fort William and arrest the NWC partners.

## In the game

- **12 June 1811.** *Lord Selkirk's grant of Assiniboia* is a **fixed** beat: flag `red_river_colony`, with a letter to the York Factor (70 days) saying settlers will come out by the Bay: "Give them every assistance; the Canadians will not like it."
- **After Seven Oaks.** If the governor is killed, a **conditional** beat fires 420 days later: Selkirk "came up the lakes with his de Meurons and took Fort William. McGillivray and the partners are under arrest" (flag `fort_william_seized`, rivalry +10) ([[William McGillivray]]).
- **Design.** Section 10.7 models his rise as an agent goal running through shares, a committee seat, a grant and a recruiting stream; section 4.4 treats the grant as fixed and the march as conditional ([[London Committee]]).

## Trivia

- The design doc dates the march to 1817; historically it was August 1816. In the game it is tied to the governor's death plus 420 days.
- The keyword list for the beat includes "de Meurons" and "soldiers".
