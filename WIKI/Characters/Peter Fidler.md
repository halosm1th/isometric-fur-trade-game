## Who they were

An HBC surveyor and trader, trained under Philip Turnor, who mapped much of the plains and was posted at Brandon House and Red River. He sheltered the colonists in 1815.

## In the game

`docs/world-content-needed.md` calls him a mapmaker and a "good companion NPC". No beat or spawned character is him. The scene does contain several **invented** Fidlers: John Fidler (clerk) at York, Mr. David Fidler (postmaster at Brunswick House), Mr. John Fidler (Manitoba House) and Mr. Charles Fidler.
