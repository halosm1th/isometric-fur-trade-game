## Where they come from

Every game day a married player's household is checked: after 300 days together, a 2 per cent daily chance of a birth. The child is a boy 52 per cent of the time. Boy names: William, James, Thomas, John, Robert; girl names: Nancy, Isabella, Margaret, Christina; surname is the father's. A headline announces "X has borne Y a son/daughter", and every member of the wife's father's household thinks 8 points better of the player ([[Marriage and Country Wives]]).

## Growing up

At each New Year everyone is a year older. A child becomes a man or woman at 16: "X is grown and takes a man's work at the post" and returns to the sheet for the work they do (`Appearance.IsChildSheet`). Between 14 and 16 they wear the adult sheet at scaled-down size.

## Why they matter

- A grown son is the player's heir if he dies: a nearer line of succession than a clerk ([[Heirs and Succession]]).
- Fathers' memories, friendships and grudges pass to sons ([[Memory and Knowledge]], [[Grudges and Vendettas]]).
- Band children are `Cree Daughter` or hunters' kin and use `cree_woman` or the nation's sheet ([[Cree Woman]]).
