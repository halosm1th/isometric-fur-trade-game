## Who they were

The dominant figure of the NWC's Montreal side. His firm, McTavish, Frobisher & Co, supplied the wintering partners, and he was a driving force in the fur trade before his death in 1804. The design notes that Simon McTavish paid for his nephews' education and placed each in a career inside his own company: nepotism as a literal recruiting pipeline.

## In the game

- **Firm name.** The Council code names the agent firm "McTavish, Frobisher & Co" and, for [[William McGillivray]]'s seat, "Montreal (McTavish, McGillivrays & Co)" ([[Councils and Accords]]).
- **Recruit pool.** Design 10.7 and 13.2 cite his nephews as the model for the NWC's kin-network hiring.
- **Namesakes.** The generated cast reuses the surname McTavish for five NWC postmasters (Peter at Nipawi, David at Fort Bas de la Riviere, Andrew at Fort Souris, Thomas at Fort Vermilion, John at Fort St. James). These are invented men, not him.
