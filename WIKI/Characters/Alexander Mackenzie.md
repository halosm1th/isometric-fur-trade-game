## Who they were

The Scottish-born Nor'Wester who reached the Arctic (1789) and the Pacific overland (1793). He later led the [[XY Company]], which merged back into the NWC in 1804.

## In the game

Only the XY paragraph in design 4.2 names him; the 1801 prologue would let the XY split develop in play. The playable window starts after 1811, so he is long retired.
