# Samuel Hearne

**Status:** Partial. Named only in an atlas note.

## Who they were

An HBC explorer who walked to the Coppermine River and founded Cumberland House in 1774, the Company's first inland post.

## In the game

The atlas entry for [[Cumberland House]] carries the note; the post is a playable site on the Saskatchewan route.
