## Daily routine

- **Not work hours:** wander the camp.
- **Fire duty** first if a hearth is low.
- **Hunts start in the morning.** After noon a hunter stays about the camp, so a long walk out would mean a night in the open.
- **Trapline first.** Check any line that has had a night; else set one if there are traps in the cache and the price makes it worth it. The chance to hunt for furs is 0.25 x pelt price (clamped 0.05-0.95): *this is the London incentive at work* ([[London Committee]], [[Trapping]]).
- **Meat need.** If the camp has under 6 days of food per mouth, the hunter goes after big game or geese at the best known site; if under 3 weeks, 30 per cent chance of laying in stock.
- **Otherwise** fish (non-winter, 30 per cent), forage (35 per cent), or wander ([[Hunting]], [[Fishing]], [[Gathering and Gardens]]).

## Combat

A hunter with a weapon is a fighter. With a bow: 30 damage, range 260, cooldown 1.6 s; with a musket: 55 damage, range 340, 4 s. Two bands at odds treat each other's hunters as enemies ([[Combat]], [[Weapons]]). A killed hunter drops standing of his band against the killer's company by 25.

## Succession

The most ambitious hunter becomes chief when the chief dies ([[Cree Chief]]).

## Tips

- Befriend a hunter early; they are the best sources of information on animal sites ([[Species Sites]]).
- A band-born player with the "Hunter" background starts Hunting 0.5, Trapping 0.35 and a Bow ([[Player Character]]).
