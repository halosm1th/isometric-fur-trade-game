# Historical People

**Status:** Partial. A handful of real people have a mechanical presence (council seats, a named Factor, namesake voyageurs, letters in campaign beats). Most are only named in letters or in the design notes. The invented cast, such as Maskwa, John Fidler or Hugh Rendall, is not historical.

> [!info] How history enters the game
> The game does not script cutscenes about named people. It runs ordered beats (`Campaigns/*.json`) that raise tension, change London's prices and send *letters* that name them, and it seeds six NWC councillors with the historical partners' names and temperaments ([[Councils and Accords]]). See [[Campaigns and Adherence]].

## Caution on names

Several scene characters share historical surnames (McTavish, Fraser, Henry, Cameron, Fidler, Riel, Cadotte). Most are invented fillers for the atlas's planned posts: use the historical-person pages, not the character names, as the source of fact.
