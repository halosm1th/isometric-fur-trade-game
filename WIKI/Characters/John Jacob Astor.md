## Who they were

The New York merchant who incorporated the American Fur Company in 1808 as sole stockholder, founded the Pacific Fur Company in 1810, and built Fort Astoria in 1811. In the War of 1812 his Columbia holdings were sold to the NWC (October 1813), and the fort became Fort George.

## In the game

- **Beat.** *Astor's Fort Astoria sold to the North West Company (Fort George)* is a fixed beat; letter from Fort William: "many of his clerks are Nor'Westers."
- **Design.** A single-principal node: one agent's goals decide everything ([[Americans]]). The 1811 Southwest Company briefly partnered him with the NWC; Congress excluded foreigners from the trade in 1816.
- **Atlas.** Fort Okanogan (1811, Pacific Fur Company) and a rival Spokane post.
