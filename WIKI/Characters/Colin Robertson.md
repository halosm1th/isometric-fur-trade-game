## Who they were

A former Nor'Wester who joined the HBC and led its Athabasca and Red River offensives from about 1815 to 1819.

## In the game

Named in the content list with John Clarke (the disastrous Athabasca expedition in which the men starved) as the HBC's counter-attack in the lawless years. No beat or NPC exists yet; the 1818-1820 "interior war" pressure beats stand in for them.
