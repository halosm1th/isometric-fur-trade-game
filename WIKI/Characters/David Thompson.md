## Who they were

A fur-trade surveyor who left the HBC for the NWC in 1797 and spent years mapping the interior. He crossed the Rockies, built Kootenay House in 1807, and reached the Columbia's mouth in July 1811, just after Astor's party. He left the West in 1812.

## In the game

- **Kootenay House** is an NWC post on the map (founded 1807) with a Kootenay family camp ([[Plateau Peoples]], [[Columbia Country]]).
