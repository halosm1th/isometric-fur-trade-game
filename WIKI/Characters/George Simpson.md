## Who they were

A young clerk who wintered in Athabasca in 1820 and was installed as Governor of the Northern Department when the HBC and the NWC united in 1821. He began a cold, efficiency-first restructuring: closing posts, laying off clerks and voyageurs (many Metis), and imposing a stricter regime. He later became governor-in-chief of Rupert's Land and was knighted. Simpson took at least two country wives, Elizabeth Sinclair (1821 to 1822) and Margaret Taylor (1825 to 1830), then went to London in 1830 and married his English cousin Frances Ramsay Simpson, setting aside his Indigenous family and helping set a company-wide fashion for European wives.

## In the game

- **The merger letter.** On 26 March 1821 the *Pemmican War* campaign sends a letter from the Governor and Committee to the York Factor: "Mr. George Simpson will govern the Northern Department. Expect economies." That is the only place he is named in code or data ([[Campaigns and Adherence]]).
- **The ending.** The design calls him "the ending" of the arc: the playable structure is the pre-1821 one, and Simpson and [[William Williams]] are epilogue material only (design 4.3, `docs/world-content-needed.md`).
- **A coda.** His biography echoes the marriage system: leaving wives behind has consequences (design 8.4).

## Trivia
