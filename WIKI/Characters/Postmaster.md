## What the role is

The master of an inland post. At Oxford House (the playable outpost) he commands three labourers and a woman (Mary Linklater) and reports to the Factor at York by sending a man down with furs and a report once a month. He runs an [[Outpost Ledger]].

At other atlas posts the same role gives each post a leader and a crew (voyageurs at NWC posts, labourers at HBC ones).

## When he dies

The post is left without a master: "Oxford House is without a master", and the steadiest labourer keeps the men together while the Factor at York sends the best-standing clerk up to take over ([[Clerk]], [[Delegation]]). If no word from Oxford House reaches York for too long, a party is sent to see how they do.

## Player route

A player who reaches standing 60 is rated *postmaster* at the ship's reckoning and receives 12 trade goods ([[Player Character]], [[Standing]]). The "Rise at the post" ambition is complete at rank postmaster.
