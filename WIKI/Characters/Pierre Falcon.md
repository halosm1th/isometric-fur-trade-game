# Pierre Falcon

## Who they were

A Metis poet and songwriter whose *La Chanson de la Grenouillere* celebrates Seven Oaks.

## In the game

`Pierre Falcon` is one of the three voyageurs at the Nor'Westers' house and a name on the brigade's list of new men ([[Voyageur]]). He is a namesake; no song or Seven Oaks role is modelled. The `Role.Voyageur` kit is Capote, Knife and 2 Pemmican.
