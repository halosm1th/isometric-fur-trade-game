## Who they are

Orkney Islanders on five-year contracts, recruited each year at Stromness. The ship lands enough new hands to bring York back to strength; each arrives with a Capote, a Knife and 2 Bread and an order to report for duty. Recruits are drawn from ten surnames: Isbister, Flett, Spence, Linklater, Corrigal, Sinclair, Setter, Tait, Garrick, Sabiston ([[Ship]], [[Hudson's Bay Company]]).

## Daily routine

- **Night (21:00-06:00)** sleep in the men's house.
- **Evening (18:00-21:00)** a talkative man drifts to the household fire (chance 0.35 + 0.55 x talkativeness); from 19:00 to 22:00 a man with rum or ale in the cache may go carousing.
- **Work hours (07:00-18:00)** carry out the day's orders ([[Daily Orders]]); with none, stoke fires (threshold 3), fetch water if the barrel holds under 6, chop wood if the woodpile is under 30, else wander the work cell.
- **Acting master.** If a post has no leader, the labourer with the highest standing gives the orders until one arrives.
- **Hangover.** A man still drunk (over 35) at first light wanders instead of working.

## Fights

A labourer with a weapon is a "fighter": he turns out against raiders if his house has the numbers, flees when outgunned and not brave, and may be sent by the Factor to relieve the Nor'Westers' stores ([[Combat]], [[Rivalry]]). The weapon statistics are on [[Weapons]].

## Tips for the player

- A player starting as an Orkney labourer gets Woodcraft 0.35 and an Axe ([[HBC Path]]).
- Rations are drawn from the Fort's stores (the "Take a ration" option draws two days) ([[Fort Ledger]]).
- Friendships made on the ship (swear to stand by Hugh Rendall and Andrew Flett) begin as bonds ([[Friends and Bonds]]).
