## Who they are

French-Canadian paddlers engaged in the parishes of Lower Canada, the muscle of the North West Company. Each year Fort William sends voyageurs north with the brigade to fill the Nor'Westers' ranks, from a list including Baptiste Lagimodiere, Pierre Falcon, Antoine Desjarlais, Joseph Cadotte, Louis Nolin, Francois Deschamps and Jean-Baptiste Lajimoniere. Their song is "A la claire fontaine"; their dream a farm at Sorel.

## Routine

Identical to the [[Labourer]]'s: sleep 21:00-06:00; evening fire; work 07:00-18:00 on the partner's orders or on firewood, water and woodpile chores. In the evening they sing a paddling song or a chorus.

## In travel

The `paddle` and `portage` animations are drawn for the brigade, where men carry cargo over a portage two pieces at a time ([[Brigade]], [[Canoe]], [[Routes and Portages]]). A brigade resolves its open water in numbers, and the portage is where things happen: hulls wear on the rocks, gum patches fix leaks, and ambushers know where you must stop.

## Fights

A voyageur with a weapon is a fighter. In the cold war between the posts they are the men the partner sends across the river to take the other house's pemmican.
