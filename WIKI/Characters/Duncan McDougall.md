# Duncan McDougall

**Status:** Partial. A seeded councillor.

| | |
|---|---|

| **Life** | c. 1777 to 1818 |

## Who they were

A partner in Astor's Pacific Fur Company who headed Fort Astoria and handed it to the North West Company in 1813, joining the NWC afterward.

## In the game

The Council seeds him as the ambitious, suspicious Athabasca councillor with two shares. The year-1811 anachronism (he was an Astorian then) is a data choice; the notable thing is the highest ambition and lowest loyalty of the six.
