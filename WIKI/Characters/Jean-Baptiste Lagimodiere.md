## Who they were

A French-Canadian freeman who settled at Red River and, with his wife Marie-Anne Gaboury, was among the colony's first families. In 1815 and 1816 he walked to Montreal with news for Selkirk. He was grandfather of Louis Riel.

## In the game

`Baptiste Lagimodiere` is a voyageur at the NWC house (the brigade list also has a spelling variant Lajimoniere). The content list calls him "a perfect courier set-piece" for the message system (design 10.1). A `Louis Riel` voyageur also appears at Lac la Ronge as a namesake only.
