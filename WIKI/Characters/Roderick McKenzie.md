# Roderick McKenzie

**Status:** Partial. A seeded councillor; the design says he had retired.

## Who they were

A cousin of [[Alexander Mackenzie]] who served at Fort Chipewyan and the Athabasca and later retired to Terrebonne. In the design's description of shares, he surrendered one of his two on retiring and the other when the XY was absorbed (5.2).

## In the game

The Council still seats him as the Lake Superior councillor with one share. Historically he is retired in 1811; the game uses him as a plausible stand-in. His 0.8 loyalty makes him the most reliably consistent voter.
