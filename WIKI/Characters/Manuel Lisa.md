## Who they were

A St. Louis fur trader who led the Missouri Fur Company up the Missouri. The Blackfeet besieged its Three Forks post in 1810.

## In the game

The atlas lists **Fort Manuel Lisa** (AMER, 1812). The design uses him with Astor as the American nuisance nodes ([[Americans]], [[John Jacob Astor]]); the siege of 1810 feeds the arming-a-side friction with the [[Blackfoot Confederacy]].
