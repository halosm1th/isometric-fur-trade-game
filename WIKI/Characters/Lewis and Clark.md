## Who they were

Captains Meriwether Lewis and William Clark led the U.S. Corps of Discovery up the Missouri to the Pacific. They wintered among the Mandan in 1804-05.

## In the game

In *The Plains Ablaze (1801-1821)* history is bent: the expedition sets out 14 May 1804, with letters from Montreal and London that it threatens western posts. On 15 September 1805 the expedition is "ambushed and slaughtered" and rumour blames British traders. If that happens, a conditional beat sends General [[James Wilkinson]] and the U.S. Army on a scorched-earth punitive expedition, and a fallout beat halts trade and provisions (rivalry and London prices respond). The 1805 date is earlier than the 1811 default start ([[Campaigns and Adherence]]).
