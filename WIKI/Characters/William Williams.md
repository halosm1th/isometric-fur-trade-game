## Who they were

The senior HBC governor in the final years. Expected to take the profitable Northern Department at the union, he instead chose the Southern Department, headquartered at Moose Factory, leaving [[George Simpson]] the North. The two departments came under one governor only in 1826.

## In the game

The design says the pre-1821 "Governor" means closer to a senior officer in a crisis than the unifying command Simpson later builds (5.1). As an epilogue beat, the Southern Department is the piece of the map Simpson does not reach in 1821 ([[Moose Factory]]).
