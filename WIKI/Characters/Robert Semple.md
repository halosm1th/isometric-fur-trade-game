## Who they were

A merchant and author who succeeded [[Miles Macdonell]] as governor of the Company's lands. He confronted Metis horsemen at Seven Oaks and was killed with about twenty colonists.

## In the game

His death is the trigger for [[Lord Selkirk]]'s conditional march: "Governor Semple is dead with twenty of the colonists, shot down at Seven Oaks by Grant's half-breeds. The colony is gone." (Norway House letter to the York Factor, 35 days). The NWC copy at Fort William: "There will be a reckoning for this in Montreal."
