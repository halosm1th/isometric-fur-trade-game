## Who they were

A physician-turned-trader who became an NWC partner, was arrested with [[William McGillivray]] when Selkirk seized Fort William in 1816, and later ran the HBC's Columbia district from Fort Vancouver.

## In the game

The content list notes him as "Fort William, Rainy Lake; later of Fort Vancouver; arrested with McGillivray". Fort Vancouver (dated 1825) is after the playable window and its camps are held back ([[Coast Peoples]]).
