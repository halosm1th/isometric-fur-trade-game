## History

The Chipewyan supplied the Churchill and Athabasca trade; the Beaver and the western Dakelh and Sekani lived by the NWC's New Caledonia posts, many of which are in the atlas with founding dates from 1806 ([[Fort Chipewyan]]).

## In play

- Bands are `CountryBand` camps with a chief, hunters and women. The scene gives the Chipewyan band a chief (Calling Otter), a hunter (Young Moose), a wife and a daughter.
- A family camp lives by a post and trades there. Posts only exist once the campaign calendar reaches their founding year, so western camps appear gradually.
- Their weekly hunt, wood and fish are computed in numbers rather than simulated tile by tile ([[Simulation Tiers]]).

## Language

The Athabaskan tongue is unrelated to every other language in the kinship table: its value is **0** with all other peoples. A player with Cree fluency learns nothing of it ([[Languages]]).
