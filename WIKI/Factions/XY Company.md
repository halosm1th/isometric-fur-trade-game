# XY Company

**Status:** Planned (design doc 4.2, 5.2). There is no `XY` faction, no XY post and no XY people in the build.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Faction value** | None |
| **Historical dates** | Founded 1798, absorbed into the NWC in 1804 |
| **Founders** | Two Montreal firms; later identified with [[Alexander Mackenzie]] |
| **Game window** | Before the 1811 start: it has already vanished |

## History

The XY Company was a splinter of the NWC's internal conflict, a Montreal rival built by merchants who felt shut out of the older partnership. It competed hard in the interior for six years, and its absorption in 1804 is what turned the NWC's share count into a hundred: **75 old NWC and 25 former XY** (design doc 5.2). Roderick Mackenzie surrendered his shares when it was absorbed.

## In the game

- The default start is 1 January 1811, seven years after the merger, so the XY Company does not exist as an actor.
- The design doc proposes an optional **1801-1811 prologue** that would seed the XY split as an initial condition of the council mechanic (Section 3.8.1 makes any splinter a general mechanic), with the 1804 amalgamation as an event inside play. That start is not built.
- The July council system ([[Councils and Accords]]) is the system that would host it: a vacated share is a prize that wintering partners compete for.

## Trivia

- The 100-share structure of the NWC after 1804 is the number the design uses to weigh votes at the council.
- No ledger, sprite or crest exists for the XY Company.
