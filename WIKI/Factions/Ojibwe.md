## History

The Ojibwe and their neighbours supplied the Great Lakes trade, the canoes and the wild rice at the Nor'Westers' Fort William and Grand Portage. The Plains Ojibwa (Saulteaux) of Red River were allies of the settlers (see [[Peguis]]).

## In play

- **Camps.** Each band is a `CountryBand` of 8 to 12 people with a chief (Ajijaak leads the Plains Ojibwa in the scene), hunters (Giizhig), a wife (Giizis) and a daughter (Memengwaa). Their cache starts with 30 dried meat, 10 pemmican, 20 firewood and 8 beaver pelts.
- **Family camps** are the "homeguard": four people each living by a post, with a cache of 2 beaver pelts, trading at that post.
- **Combat.** Only hunters carrying weapons fight (musket 55 damage; bow 30) ([[Combat]]).
- **Starting as one.** A band-born character gets kin from the start; Ojibwe and Metis characters begin with Cree fluency of 0.6 ([[Languages]]).

## Languages

Ojibwe and Cree are close enough to trade in (0.6). Ojibwe and Metis (Michif) reach 0.5. Otherwise nothing is understood.
