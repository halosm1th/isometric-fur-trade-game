## Notes

The faction bundles the farming villages (Mandan, Hidatsa, Arikara) with neighbouring horse nations (Crow, Cheyenne, Shoshone) and the Ho-Chunk, because the game has only one slot for the region. Each band is still its own ledger with its own people.

- **The Mandan camp** has a chief (White Antelope), a hunter (Calling Moose), a wife (Red Bird Woman) and a daughter (Big Berry Woman) in the scene.
- **Earth-lodge villages.** The location art `loc-earth-lodge-village.png` shows the intended look of their settlement ([[Terrain and Biomes]]).
- **Language.** Mandan and Assiniboine or Dakota reach 0.3; otherwise nothing.
- **The Plains Ablaze** campaign names Fort Mandan in its news: Lewis and Clark set out in May 1804 and, in this alternate history, their command is ambushed and slaughtered on the upper river in September 1805 ([[Lewis and Clark]]).
