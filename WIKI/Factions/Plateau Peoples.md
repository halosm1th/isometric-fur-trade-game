## History

[[David Thompson]] built Kootenay House in 1807, the first Nor'Westers' post in this country, and reached the Columbia's mouth on 15 July 1811. Spokane House (NWC, 1810) stood beside a rival Pacific Fur Company post in 1812 ([[Americans]]).

## In play

- Posts here are date-gated: Fort Kamloops (1812) and others appear as the calendar reaches them.
- The scene gives the Kootenay band a chief (Two Marten), hunter (Spotted Wolf), wife and daughter.
- Fights follow the same rules as other nations: armed male hunters turn out ([[Combat]]).
- The "Plateau tongue" is not related to any other language in the table (kinship 0 with all).
