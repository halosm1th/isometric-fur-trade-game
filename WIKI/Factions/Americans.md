## Design intent

A deliberately thin "nuisance faction". Two background nodes of different shape:

- **Astor / New York.** The American Fur Company (1808) belonged to [[John Jacob Astor]] alone, so one agent's goals decide everything it does. His Pacific Fur Company built Fort Astoria in 1811 and sold it to the NWC in October 1813.
- **St. Louis.** A small merchant association: [[Manuel Lisa]]'s Missouri Fur Company (1809). The Blackfeet besieged its Three Forks post in 1810, feeding the arming-a-side friction between nations.
- **The law.** Congress excluded foreigners from the American trade in 1816.
- **No blockade.** Americans trade freely while the Continental Blockade hurts HBC and NWC.

As background AI they would poach trap lines, compete for trade partners, and cause occasional violence around the Columbia and upper Missouri.

## What exists today

- **Atlas posts.** Fort Okanogan (1811, Pacific Fur Company), Fort Manuel Lisa (1812) are in-window. Fort Union (1828), Fort Clark (1831), Fort Piegan (1831), Fort McKenzie (1832), Fort Berthold (1845) and Fort Benton (1846) are later; the campaign gates posts by founding year, so those never appear in a 1811-1821 game ([[All Trading Posts]]).
- **Campaign beats.** *The Pemmican War* has the War of 1812 (18 Jun 1812, news from Montreal and London) and "Astor's Fort Astoria sold to the North West Company" (16 Oct 1813).
- **The Plains Ablaze.** The alternative campaign (`Campaigns/Gemini and Clark.json`) is built around [[Lewis and Clark]] and a punitive U.S. Army invasion of the Plains under General [[James Wilkinson]].

## Trivia

- The 1811 Southwest Company briefly partnered Astor with the NWC.
- Many Pacific Fur Company clerks became Nor'Westers after 1813 (design doc 10.7 recruit pool).
