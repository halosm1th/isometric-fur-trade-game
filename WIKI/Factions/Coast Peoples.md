## In play

All four family camps sit at posts founded after the game's window (Fort Langley 1827, and Fort Vancouver, Fort Nisqually and Fort Victoria later), so in the 1811-1821 historical campaign they are held back by the founding-year gate and stay empty. The one band camp (Coast Salish) exists from the start, with a chief (Spotted Hawk), hunter (Young Raven) and women.

The design doc notes that the Columbia and Oregon country carry "Chinook and other coastal/river nations, and the first trickle of American settlers" (6). Fort George (Astoria, 1811, ceded 1813) is the in-period Columbia anchor ([[Americans]], [[John Jacob Astor]]).

## Trivia

Coast people have the fewest atlas camps of any nation (one band), and the Coast Salish daughters are drawn on the shared `cree_woman` sheet because no separate women's sheet has been made for any nation but the Metis.
