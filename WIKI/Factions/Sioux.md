## In play

The Dakota (Santee) camp has a chief (Hinhan), a hunter (Spotted Horse), a wife (Grey Moon Woman) and a daughter (Calling Willow Woman) in the scene. The Lakota camp lies far south, near the Missouri.

- **No posts.** The Sioux have no homeguard families because there are no posts in their country within the window.
- **Hunting parties.** The design doc names "a Sioux hunting party" as the example of a goal-driven party that can collide with the player's (15.1); that collision model is planned.
- **Language.** Dakota and Assiniboine (Nakoda) are two Siouan dialects: 0.8 kinship. Dakota and Mandan reach 0.3 ([[Languages]]).

## Trivia

The Assiniboine named in the York cast bear Dakota names (Tchatka, Mato, Wiyaka). The Assiniboine broke from the Yanktonai Dakota long before the game's date.
