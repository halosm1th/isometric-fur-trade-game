## History

Plains buffalo hunters and provision suppliers, the Assiniboine came down to the Bay to trade pemmican for goods and sometimes fought the Blackfoot alongside the Cree. In the design, every gun a post sells tips the balance of the intertribal war (design doc 7.2).

## The band at York

Eight people: the chief **Tchatka**, hunters **Mato**, **Wanbdi**, **Cetan**, women **Wiyaka**, **Ptesan**, **Wakanda** and a daughter **Winona**. The names are Dakota, a placeholder (the game's own comment says so for the Cree names).

- **Opening stores:** 40 Pemmican, 6 Caribou Hide, 20 Firewood, 4 Beaver Pelt, 3 Wolf Pelt, 1 Kettle, 10 Water and 6 Trade Goods.
- **The seasonal round.** On **1 July** the band comes down the river road and pitches its tipis on the trail below York Factory; on **1 September** it goes back up-country for the buffalo ([[Assiniboine Camps]]).
- **Pemmican weekly.** While at winter ground it adds 14 Pemmican and 1 Caribou Hide each week. Anything over the larger of 30 or three per mouth is sold: lots of up to 20 are taken to the post that will have them, at the post's price for goods.
- **The chief** takes a trade trip when he has provisions to sell (12 more than six per mouth) as well as when the pelt load or hunger drives him ([[Cree Chief]]).
- **The Cree** are old allies: standing between the bands starts at 65 and mends 0.3 a day. Below 25 their hunters treat each other as enemies in the bush.

## Language

Dakota is 0.8 intelligible to an Assiniboine speaker; Mandan only 0.3; everything else nothing. A player who does not speak the tongue sees speech bubbles replaced with "(Assiniboine - you catch a word or two)" ([[Languages]]).
