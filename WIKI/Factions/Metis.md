## History

The Metis are the economic engine that feeds both companies, via pemmican and the buffalo hunt. Communal hunts were organised almost like military campaigns, with hunt captains, scouts and formation discipline (design doc 5.3, 16.1). Freemen (independent traders) sit outside the hierarchy: the most freedom, the least institutional backing. They are the natural interpreters between HBC's one-way monologue and NWC's negotiated partnership.

## In play

- **A hunt captain leads each camp.** The scene's Forks band is led by **Antoine Hogue (hunt captain)** with a hunter, Michel Bruce, and Marguerite Hogue (a woman in the country-wife dress). The Metis chief wears the voyageur sheet.
- **Combat.** The hunters carry muskets (the scene gives them Musket, Knife, Capote and 2 Pemmican). Most other bands start with bows, so the Forks hunters out-gun them ([[Combat]]).
- **Supply.** Pemmican is the Metis trade good; the Pemmican Proclamation threatens their livelihood ([[Red River Colony]]).
- **Language kinship.** Michif is "half Cree": 0.8 with Cree, 0.5 with Ojibwe.
- **Starting.** The NWC path offers "Freeman's son" (Metis, son of a freeman and a Cree mother): Hunting 0.45, Cree fluency 0.8, kin in the Cree band ([[NWC Path]]). A character may also choose any Metis camp in the band-born path.
