# York Boat

**Status:** Planned (design doc section 12). The sprite exists (`assets/sprites/vehicles/york_boat.png`, 400x130) but nothing in the code loads it: there is no York boat object, no York-boat brigade and no boat-building. "All three unwired vehicles correspond to transport modes in design doc section 12 that the code does not model yet." (`docs/asset-inventory.md`)

![[vehicle-york_boat.png|420]]

The York boat is the Hudson's Bay Company's answer to the canoe: a large, heavy open boat that carried two to three times as much as a north canoe for the same crew. It became the Company's workhorse on the inland route out of York Factory.

## Design facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Length** | up to 46 ft |
| **Crew** | 6 to 8 |
| **Cargo** | up to about 13,000 lb for the largest (2 to 3 times a canot du nord's) |
| **Portage** | Too heavy to carry: the crew cut a path, lay rollers and drag it overland |
| **Origin** | Largely a Métis invention (William Sinclair's employees; mainly crewed by Métis boatmen) |
| **Rise** | Dominant HBC transport from the early 1820s: new technology as the game nears 1821 |

Consequences for play (design doc 12.2): a canoe brigade caught at a portage can grab the boat and run; a York-boat brigade is stuck dragging a multi-ton vessel through brush for as long as it takes, a much longer and more dangerous ambush window. It is also a natural specialisation hook for the Métis path.

## What exists in the game today

- The art, ready to place.
- A **boat-building shed** outside York's palisade (part of the post survey layout).
- In code, Rock Portage on the Hayes road is described as "the depot where the York boats gave way to canoes", so everything was transshipped there as well as carried over: two days. See [[Brigade]].
- `docs/world-content-needed.md` lists "Red River cart, dog sled, toboggan, York boat (as owned transport)" under items, and the boatbuilder among the tradesmen missing from posts.
