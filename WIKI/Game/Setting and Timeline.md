# Setting and Timeline

**Status:** Partial. The 1811-1821 arc is implemented as dated campaign beats ([[Campaigns and Adherence]]); the 1801-1811 prologue era and the post-1821 epilogue are design-document ideas only.

The game is set in the Canadian fur trade during the **Pemmican War**, the years of open rivalry between the [[Hudson's Bay Company]] and the [[North West Company]]. Play begins in 1811 at **York Factory** on Hudson Bay (about 57 N, which sets the length of the days; see [[Calendar and Seasons]]) and the world reaches west across the Saskatchewan and Athabasca countries to the Columbia and south to the Missouri villages. See [[World Map]] and [[Regions]].

## The arc, 1811-1821

The table gives the real historical shape the historical campaign script follows (dates are the ones in `Campaigns/historical.json`).

| Date | Event | Kind in the script |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Jun 1811 | The Continental Blockade holds the fur market down (London's price and quota orders drop to 85%) | Fixed |
| 12 Jun 1811 | Lord Selkirk's grant of Assiniboia: the [[Red River Colony]] | Fixed |
| 18 Jun 1812 | War between Britain and the United States | Fixed |
| 16 Oct 1813 | Astor's Fort Astoria sold to the North West Company (becomes Fort George) | Fixed |
| 8 Jan 1814 | Governor Miles Macdonell's **Pemmican Proclamation** | Event |
| 30 May 1814 | Peace in Europe: the fur market reopens | Fixed |
| 25 Jun 1815 | The Red River Colony attacked and dispersed | Event |
| 19 Jun 1816 | The **Battle of Seven Oaks**: Governor Semple and twenty colonists killed | Event |
| about 420 days after Seven Oaks | [[Lord Selkirk]] marches on Fort William and arrests the partners | Conditional |
| 1 Jun 1818 / 1819 / 1820 | The lawless years: posts burned, brigades stopped | Pressure |
| 26 Mar 1821 | The union of the Hudson's Bay and North West Companies | Event |

The ending belongs to **George Simpson**, who governs the Northern Department of the combined company and, in the design document's telling, "begins ruthlessly restructuring" it, laying off clerks and voyageurs. Whatever path you took, the 1821 merger measures what you have become (see [[The Reckoning of 1821]]).

## Fixed versus emergent history

Per [[Design Principles]], the timeline is not a script the world plays regardless. A small core is fixed because it sits outside anyone's reach: the blockade, the colony's founding, and the 1821 merger being structurally inevitable. Everything else (the Proclamation, Seven Oaks, the interior war) is *pressure* the world may or may not answer. One rule sits between: if the colony is destroyed and its governor killed, Selkirk comes in response, whenever that happens. The **adherence** setting lets the player decide how hard history is held to.

## Who is in the world

- The two companies: the [[Hudson's Bay Company]] (York Factory, Oxford House, Moose Factory and the posts of the Bay) and the [[North West Company]] (Fort William and the interior). The brief [[XY Company]] rivalry is background.
- The nations: [[Cree]], [[Assiniboine]], [[Ojibwe]], [[Dene]], [[Blackfoot Confederacy]], [[Sioux]], [[Plateau Peoples]], [[Coast Peoples]] and the [[Missouri Villages]], plus the [[Metis]] of the Red River.
- The [[Americans]] as a nuisance faction (Lewis and Clark, Astor), the [[London Committee]], and the [[Ship's Crew]].
- Historical people such as [[Historical People|Selkirk, Simpson, McGillivray, Thompson and Grant]].

## The optional earlier era

The design document describes an optional 1801-1811 prologue (the tail of the XY Company rivalry, Lewis and Clark's journey, Astor assembling the Pacific Fur Company) and a post-1821 epilogue (Simpson's country wives and his 1830 marriage to a cousin). Only the first is hinted at in the code: the second shipped campaign, **The Plains Ablaze (1801-1821)**, contains Lewis-and-Clark beats dated 1804-1805. See [[Campaigns and Adherence]].
