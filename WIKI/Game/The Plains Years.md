# The Plains Years

**Status:** Planned. Full spec: `docs/specs/story-plains-years.md`. A proposed campaign arc, not in the game.

An HBC hand lands at [[York Factory]], reports to [[William Auld]], is sent inland to a plains post (suggested: Brandon House), slowly makes friends in the nearby bands, is opposed by the Nor'Westers next door, is drawn into the buffalo and horse country, and is eventually asked to take part in, supply, or try to end a war between Indigenous nations. It ends in the union of 1821 ([[The Reckoning of 1821]]).
