Blurb: *You are of the band that hunts the lowlands and trades at the Bay. Your family, your hunting grounds, and two companies who want your furs.*

![[loc-cree-camp.png|420]]

## Starting kit and kin

Your pack is cleared. You carry a hide coat, snowshoes, knife, 4 dried meat, flint, fishing line, 2 firewood and 2 steel traps, plus whatever your background and traits add. The first three living members of the band's household list (the leader is not excluded) become your kin at regard 50. Each has two shared memories with you, so they count as friends under [[Friends and Bonds]] from the first hour.

The nephew background binds the band's leading man (**Maskwa**) at regard 45.

## The prologue: "Morning in the camp"

Opening line: *The Cree summer camp by the Bay men's house. These are your people.* The text names the season and says the lodges "stand on the trail by the Bay men's house for the trading". If the world date puts the band in winter, they "stand in the winter camp in the bush". Smoke, dogs, children at the river.

Your **elder** (the first living man of your kin) is outside his lodge with the winter's furs bundled at his feet. Your **cousin** is restringing a bow. **A Canadian from across the river** has come into the camp with a keg.

### Sit with the elder
*"Take these down to the Bay house. Four good beaver. Bring back powder and a kettle. The master will try to give you less than they're worth, and the Canadians will try to give you rum. Don't let either of them make a fool of you."*
- **Take the furs:** +4 beaver pelts in your pack; elder's regard +5; a **TradeForKin favour** (due in 8 days) is opened with him. This is your first [[Favours|favour]]: deliver a kettle with no beaver left in your pack (i.e. sell the furs, buy the kettle at the post) and hand it to him to settle it. Delivering it earns +15 regard and a "good turn" he owes you. If the day passes with it undone, he remembers it and your regard falls by 8.

### Go with your cousin to look at the snares
Two hares in the snares and a third torn loose by a fox. He talks about a girl in the other band and whether her father would ever look at him.
- **Promise to speak for him:** cousin's regard +10, a shared memory ("checked the snares together and talked of marriage"). A seed for [[Marriage and Country Wives]].
- **Tease him:** +3 regard.

### See what the Canadian wants
*"The partner sends his compliments. There's more where this came from for a man who brings his furs across the river instead of to the Bay house."*
- **Drink with him:** you gain and immediately drink one rum. A journal note records the Nor'Wester's offer.
- **Send him away:** elder's regard +4.

### Go about the day
Closes the scene. Nothing is forced; you are free to play.

## Tips

> [!tip]
> The four beaver are worth more at one counter than the other depending on the price. Compare the Company's price (the Factor's directive) with the Nor'Westers' before you walk. See [[Fur Prices]] and [[Trading and Barter]].

> [!info]
> Born to the band, the Company's [[Standing]] ladder does not apply. Your progress is the band's standing with the Company (0-100; they trade only while above 20) and your regard with individuals.

> [!info]
> The **Another people** option uses the same scene with different names: the elder, a woman of the lodge and a cousin are spawned for any camp out in the country that has nobody named in it, and the "trader's man" belongs to that camp's own post. See [[Starting Paths]].
