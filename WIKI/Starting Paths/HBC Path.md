The game's blurb: *You come ashore from the annual ship, a day out from the Factory, with a letter for the Factor and a contract for five years. Rich, slow, and bound by London's orders a year late.*

![[map-york-factory-game.png|500]]

## Starting kit

Everyone gets the new-hand kit: 4 pemmican, 2 dried goose, 4 firewood, capote, knife, **6 trade goods**, 3 steel traps, kettle, 2 water, flint, tumpline, axe, fishing line. Backgrounds and traits add to it (see [[Starting Paths]]).

## The prologue: "The last day of the crossing"

Opening line: *York Factory, Hudson's Bay. You are the Company's new hand.* You begin on the ship's deck, out in the roads. Two Orkney lads who signed on with you at Stromness stand beside you. If the world was already running and the ship is not arriving or docked, the scene becomes "The last day aboard the sloop" and the mate is "the master".

![[vehicle-ship.png|300]]

The scene is a hub. Each of three conversations can be taken once; "Turn in" ends the day.

### Talk with Hugh and Andrew
**Hugh Rendall** and **Andrew Flett** are labourers aged 17-23 (capote, knife, 2 bread). Hugh talks of his mother's croft on Sanday and "the five years ahead of him". Andrew says little but shares his tobacco.

- **"We will." Shake on it.** Both gain +15 regard for you and a second shared memory (+12 each). Combined with the memory from landing they now clear the friend threshold (regard 20 and bond 3). See [[Friends and Bonds]].
- **Keep your own counsel.** Nothing changes. You still start with +12 regard and a shared memory each from "crossing together".

![[char-labourer.png|110]]

### See what Davy Gunn wants
Davy Gunn, a sailor, offers brandy of his own: *"The Indians pay three beaver a keg and the Company need never know. Two pieces of your goods for each of my kegs."*

![[char-sailor.png|110]]

- **Buy two kegs (4 pieces of goods).** You lose 4 trade goods and gain **6 rum**. The two lads and anyone aboard now remember (weight 1.2) that you "traded on your own account aboard the ship". If the Factor hears this, it is exactly the kind of story that [[Standing]] punishes at the ship's sailing (the Factor's report: -4 plus 3 per instance, capped at 20). If you have fewer than 4 goods he sneers and the choice fails.
- **No.** Nothing.
- **Tell the mate.** Standing **+3**. "The Company will think well of it; the crew will not." Davy looks at you across the deck for the rest of the day.

### Ask the mate (or master) about the Factory
The mate names the Factor ("an old Bay man, hard but fair"), warns you about the **rival clerk** (the most ambitious living clerk at the Factory; see [[Rivalry]]) and about the Nor'Westers' partner across the river ("bad blood since the winter"). Taking it adds a journal note: report at once, the rival wants the next place, keep clear of the Canadians.

### Turn in
Morning: the ship comes up the Hayes on the tide and makes fast. *Palisade, flag, smoke from the chimneys, and every soul in the place come down to the shore.* **Step ashore** puts you on the planks of the wharf (the code finds the nearest dry cell so you cannot start in the river) and logs: *You came ashore at York Factory with Hugh and Andrew. Report to Mr. Auld.*

![[loc-york-factory-wharf.png|400]]

## First task

`Report` (need 1): "find Mr. Auld and speak to him". Talking to the Factor completes it and awards **+3 standing**. Until you do, the Factor's morning orders skip you. The two lads have a `ReportForDuty` goal and will go to him on their own. After the report, the [[Delegation]] system hands you ordinary work (firewood, ship cargo, a letter to the chief).

![[char-factor.png|110]] ![[char-clerk.png|110]]

## Tips

> [!tip]
> Telling the mate costs you nothing mechanical but Davy and his shipmates' goodwill. Buying the brandy gives you 6 rum to use as a gift (rum doubles the gain from a gift) but plants a private-trade story that can come back at the sailing. See [[Gossip, Lies and Leverage]].

> [!info]
> The Orkney labourer arrives with a tumpline and axe, but the *Country-born* background gives you Cree 0.7 and a relative in the Cree band, which makes [[Trading and Barter]] with the band much easier.
