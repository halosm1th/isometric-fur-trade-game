# Global Trade

**Status:** Planned. Full spec: `docs/specs/global-trade.md`. Nothing on this page is in the game yet.

Today furs leave the map on the ship and London's only reaction is a ratio against quota ([[London Committee Orders]]). Nothing outside Rupert's Land has a price, a stock or a mood, there is no money, and a kettle costs the same at [[York Factory]] as at [[Edmonton House]].

## What is planned

- **Distance gradient** (first): goods cost more and furs pay less the further a post is from its depot, along the existing brigade routes ([[Brigade]]).
- **A market with two prices**: the *standard of trade* (goods per [[Made Beaver]], as now) and a *market price* in sterling at London, Montreal, New York and beyond.
- **A shock calendar**: the Continental Blockade, the War of 1812, the 1814 peace and the 1816 exclusion act as dated multipliers ([[Setting and Timeline]]).
- **Cargo in flight** and **company cash**, so solvency and the 1821 merger come from the books, not a date ([[The Reckoning of 1821]]).
- **The Trade Ledger**: a journal page showing last-known prices with their age (news travels by letter and ship), routes, the company's books and the value of the player's pack at each node.
