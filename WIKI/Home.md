# Isometric Fur Trade Game Wiki

![[ui-menu-background.jpg|700]]

Welcome to the unofficial wiki for **Isometric Fur Trade Game**: an isometric, open-world simulation RPG of the fur trade wars of 1811–1821. Play one person in a vast, indifferent country between the **Hudson's Bay Company** and the **North West Company**, among nations who were here first. Trade, winter, marry, scheme, get caught. You will die, and your family will remember.

Every person in the world is a real agent with needs, memories and goals, and the player is just one of them. Nothing is scripted: news has to travel, and the 1821 merger is the reckoning rather than the goal. Start with [[Game Overview]] and [[Design Principles]].
