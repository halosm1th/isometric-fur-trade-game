## What is there

Mr. Charles Hodgson (the master) and two labourers, James Halcro and George Clouston. The house is a single room: hearth, shelf, tall shelf, fur bale, table, candle stand and five beds, with a woodpile at (9,6) and a crate at (10,3). The sign reads *Albany Fort / Hudson's Bay Company*. Stock at the start: 25 pemmican, 20 dried meat, 25 firewood, 40 trade goods, 8 tobacco, 10 water, an axe, 2 fishing lines and 4 beaver pelts; each week a hunt adds dried meat (7 a mouth in summer, 5 in winter).

The post is surrounded by wildlife sites named for it: goose marshes, hare flats, ptarmigan flats, beaver lodges and a woodland caribou herd (see [[Species Sites]]), good for the [[Hunting]] of the autumn flight.

## Role

The design doc places Albany inside the Southern Department with Moose Factory, Rupert House and Eastmain House (the latter two are not on the map). The Albany River also leads inland to Henley House, Gloucester House, Martin Falls and Osnaburgh House (see [[Hudson Bay Lowlands]]).
