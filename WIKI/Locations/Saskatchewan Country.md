## Description

The Saskatchewan is the broad road into the plains. [[Cumberland House]] (1774, Hearne's first inland post) is the gateway; upriver a chain of paired posts follows the river: The Pas, Nipawi, Carlton House and South Branch House, Hudson House and Manchester House (both given up before 1811), Fort George and Buckingham House (both closed 1800), Fort Vermilion, [[Edmonton House]] and Fort Augustus, and in the foothills Rocky Mountain House and Acton. Many of these are NWC and HBC pairs facing each other across a palisade.

Here the design doc's "plains" feel is sharpest: **Blackfoot Confederacy** horse country, the Plains Cree, and the Stoney (Nakoda). Chesterfield House, at the Red Deer forks (1800 to 1802), is a short-lived HBC attempt to reach the south; Carlton House is the atlas's "key provisioning depot".

## Peoples and camps

Camps in this region: the Blackfoot, Blood, Peigan, Sarcee and Gros Ventre bands (the [[Blackfoot Confederacy]]), the Plains and Woodland Cree, the Nakoda (Stoney), and family camps at each post. See [[Country Camps]] for the full list.

## Wildlife

By this wiki's grouping: 3 buffalo plains, 20 hare flats, 21 beaver lodges and 9 wolf dens. Buffalo plains such as "Buffalo plain (Blackfoot)" and "Buffalo plain (Cypress Hills)" supply the plains bands.

## Tips

- Heavy load slows a canoe; the brigade route is 1,344 km.
- Blackfoot family camps stand beside Edmonton House, Fort Augustus, Fort George, Buckingham House and Fort Vermilion.
- Expect NWC and HBC rivals side by side: tension is local.
