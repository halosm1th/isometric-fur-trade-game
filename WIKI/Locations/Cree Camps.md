# Cree Camps

**Status:** Implemented. The Cree band at York Factory is a full simulated community that follows the seasons; the wider country has more Cree bands and homeguard families as lighter-weight camps (see [[Country Camps]]).

## The camp

Each camp is a ring of **five tipis** placed at (3,0), (0,3), (-3,0), (0,-3) and (3,3) from a central **campfire** (a hearth with 4 hours of fuel that burns in the open), with a woodpile at (2,-2). The cache (`AccessCell`) is one cell beside the fire. Painted with the tipi, campfire and woodpile tiles (see [[Props and Furniture]]).

Eleven people in all. Death is permanent: a dead chief is succeeded by a hunter.

## The seasonal round

- **5 June, Breakup.** The band strikes the winter camp and pitches the tipis at the summer camp on the trail beside the post for the trading season. The cells, the campfire hearth, the cache, the woodpile and everyone's bed and work cell move, and clients get the repaint.
- **10 November, Freeze-up.** They go back to the winter camp in the bush.
- A game that starts mid-season puts the band where the season would have it (5 June to 9 November at the summer camp).

## Trade and standing

Starting cache: 30 venison, 20 dried goose, 20 firewood, 6 beaver pelts, 4 trade goods, 8 steel traps, 2 kettles, 12 water and 4 fishing lines. The band's **standing with the Company** starts at 50 and with the Nor'Westers at 50; it will not trade with a post once the standing is 20 or less. A hungry band blames the Company (-3 a day if the price is poor, -1 otherwise); fed and fairly paid, it improves by up to +1 a day. Killing one of their number costs -25 with the killer's company. Their chief chooses each trip where to sell: price times how the band feels about the company, divided by the walk.

The band knows the country within about 600 km (every wildlife site in range), so asking a hunter "about the hunting" is the way to learn where game lies (rapport 15). Cree hunters range to the sites more often when the Company's price is high.

## Player notes

- The [[Cree Path]] starts here.
- Marrying into the band (see [[Marriage and Country Wives]]) means the band's kin look for gifts each 6 June.
- The Assiniboine camp near the Cree summer camp is on friendly terms (standing 65) but can slide into being "at odds" below 25; carrying tobacco between camps (+15) mends it.
