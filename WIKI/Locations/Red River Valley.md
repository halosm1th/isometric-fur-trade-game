## Description

The Red River country is the game's war zone. It is the **pemmican belt**: the NWC's Assiniboine and Qu'Appelle posts (Pine Fort, Montagne a la Bosse, Fort Souris, Fort Esperance, Portage la Prairie, Fort Alexandria) and the HBC's Brandon House, Manitoba House and Fort Pelly fed the brigades of the whole fur country, and the Métis buffalo hunters supplied them. Lord Selkirk's colony of Assiniboia at the Forks (see [[Red River Colony]]) set the two companies' provisioning against each other, and the campaign's beats run straight through it: the grant (12 June 1811), the Pemmican Proclamation (8 January 1814), the colony dispersed (25 June 1815) and Seven Oaks (19 June 1816).

Design doc §5.3 gives a Métis/Freeman start at Red River (Upper Fort Garry is dated 1822 and so cannot be the start; Fort Douglas or Fort Gibraltar are the in-window choices). The region also includes the north end of Lake Winnipeg, where [[Norway House]] (1814) is the HBC's inland depot, and Lake of the Woods country to the east belongs to [[Great Lakes]].

## Peoples and camps

Camps in this region: the Assiniboine band (cell 471, 1529), the Métis of the Forks, Pembina and the Qu'Appelle, the Plains Ojibwa (Saulteaux) and the family camps at each post. See [[Country Camps]] for the full list.

## Wildlife

Buffalo country: by this wiki's grouping the region has about 4 buffalo plains, 29 hare flats, 17 beaver lodges and a handful of wolf dens. The Métis hunters and the Assiniboine run the summer (15 June) and fall (15 October) bison hunts.

## Tips

- Both companies' pemmican posts sit within a day or two of each other; whichever house your band sells to decides who eats.
- Expect interception: tension rises toward the Seven Oaks beat (+15 pressure, +25 on the day).
- Don't call a pre-1822 Red River post "Fort Garry".
