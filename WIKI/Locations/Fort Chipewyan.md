## People

Mr. Andrew Shaw (partner), David Leith (clerk), four voyageurs (Alexis Gingras, Basile Nolin, Jean-Baptiste Dumas, Pierre Beaulieu) and Margaret Allard. Invented cast.

## Role

Ninth in the layout priority list ("Fort Chipewyan / Fort Wedderburn (Athabasca)"). `world-content-needed.md` notes the HBC's Fort Wedderburn (1815, beside it) would be a centre of the 1815 to 1821 violence but is not on the map. The Northern (Athabasca) brigade route ends here after crossing Methye Portage (see [[Routes and Portages]]). Lake Athabasca and the Caribou herds of the "Fort Chipewyan country" (several named sites) lie round it.
