# Locations

**Status:** Partial. One place is a complete, playable world (York Factory and its country); the rest of the 105-entry map is data, plus six custom post layouts.

![[map-world-overview.png|700]]

The game's map runs from the Pacific to James Bay and Sault Ste. Marie at 40 px per kilometre. Only the first road (York Factory to Oxford House) and its neighbours are built in depth. Start with the [[World Map]], then the [[Regions]].

## Quick facts

- Distances use 40 world px per km; York Factory to Oxford House is 316 km by the traced road.
- The ship reaches York on 20 August and sails on 15 September.
- Posts appear by founding year in the historical campaign: nine are founded in play (1812 to 1821); 25 dated after 1821 never appear in the window.
