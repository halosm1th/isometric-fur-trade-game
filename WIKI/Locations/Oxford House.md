## Description

One house that is store, counter and bunkhouse in one: the hearth at (1,1), a shelf, a tall shelf, a fur bale, a table, a candle stand and five beds. Inside the palisade stand the post's woodpile (9,6), a crate (10,3) and a fur bale (1,7). The sign at the gate reads *Oxford House / Hudson's Bay Company*. The Assiniboine's winter camp lies about 45 km away on the upland near the post (cell 639, 920).

Near it are two rich beaver lodges (Knee Lake, 50/70, and Oxford, 55/75), a herd of caribou the post knows from the start, and the wolf den (moved south for this post). It lives on ice-fished food in March: it is issued three fishing lines.

## The books

Starting stock: 30 pemmican, 30 dried goose, 30 firewood, **50 trade goods**, 2 muskets, 8 steel traps, 1 kettle, 10 water, 2 axes, 2 snowshoes, 3 fishing lines and 8 beaver pelts. Once a week an **inland band party** trades here abstractly (furs for goods at the price); with bare shelves they go on to the Canadians and the furs count as lost. When the stores run short the postmaster buys provisions off the country.

## The river road

The road runs gate to gate from [[York Factory]], 34 traced points following the Hayes and fording it where it must. Along the road, in upstream order:

| Carry | At | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rock Portage | 0.404 of the way, about 128 km from York | The depot where York boats gave way to canoes; the heavy portage, two days |
| Borwick's Falls | 0.531 | On the Hill River stretch |
| Trout Falls | 0.813 | Between Knee Lake and Oxford Lake |

Hill's Gates and Robinson Portage are *above* Oxford House on the way to [[Norway House]] and are not on this road. A brigade is vulnerable at the carries; see [[Routes and Portages]] and [[Brigade]].

Every month the postmaster sends a man down with furs and a report (news of furs, food days, men fit); the Factor reads it and updates what he believes about the post. After the ship sails the Factor sends the fall outfit up. If Oxford House loses its master the men carry on under the steadiest labourer until the Factor, once he hears, names the best clerk to the charge and sends him up the road.

## Player notes

- **Posted inland.** Volunteer to the Factor ("Send me up to Oxford House with the outfit", standing 12): you get ten pieces, a letter for Sutherland and the *Courier* task. You are then "posted inland" and Sutherland gives your morning orders.
- **Travel mode.** At the road head below either gate, E starts travel mode: you march waypoint to waypoint while the clock runs at 6x. About 13 game hours York to Oxford House by the project's notes.
- **Dev start.** `--spawn=outpost` or `--spawn=road`.
