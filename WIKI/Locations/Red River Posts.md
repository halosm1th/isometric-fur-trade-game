# Red River Posts

**Status:** Partial. Fort Gibraltar has a custom plan and an 8-person cast; Fort Douglas and the rest are generic posts. Battle sites (Seven Oaks) are not placed.

![[map-region-red-river.png|500]]

The meeting of the Red and Assiniboine rivers, "the Forks", is where the Pemmican War is fought. The map gives three pairs of posts and a ring of pemmican and provision posts round them.

**If you add a Red River start, don't call it Fort Garry** (`world-content-needed.md` §4.1): for 1811-1821 it should be Fort Douglas or Fort Gibraltar, or a Métis camp at Pembina/Fort Daer (not on the map).

### Fort Gibraltar

Palisade 18 x 14, gate at offset 8, top corner at cell 635, 1573 (place cell 625, 1591; 97.16 W, 49.88 N). Buildings: the master's house (2,2; 5 x 6), **the pemmican store** (9,2; a Warehouse, 8 x 6), the trading room (1,8; a Depot, 7 x 5) and the men's house (10,8; 7 x 5). Props: flagstaff (8,9) and a drying rack (16,12). The cast: Mr. Hugh Ross, Donald Campbell (clerk), four voyageurs (Michel Beaulieu, Alexis Lambert, Antoine Paradis, Antoine Delorme) and two women (Isabella Paradis, Jane Primeau). A Métis family camp lies beside it (cell 630, 1601), and the Métis band "of the Forks" is at cell 633, 1638.

## Events

Campaign beats put these places at the heart of the war: Lord Selkirk's grant of Assiniboia (12 Jun 1811), the Pemmican Proclamation (8 Jan 1814), the colony dispersed (25 Jun 1815) and **Seven Oaks** (19 Jun 1816). News of each goes by courier from Norway House to the Factor at York (see [[Campaigns and Adherence]], [[Red River Colony]], [[The Reckoning of 1821]]). The Seven Oaks battlefield (Frog Plain) itself is listed as a wanted "battle site" and is not a place yet.
