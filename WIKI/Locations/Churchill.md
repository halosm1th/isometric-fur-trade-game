## What is there

Mr. John Heron (master) and two labourers, Adam Sabiston and Magnus Sabiston. The surroundings carry goose marshes, hare and ptarmigan flats, caribou herds and wolf dens named for the post (see [[Species Sites]]).

The HBC start can begin aboard the Company's *sloop from Churchill* when the supply ship is not yet in the roads (see [[HBC Path]]): "three hands Churchill could spare".
