## What it is in the game

Two things share the name. The **post** (1778 to 1803) is the NWC's earlier Superior depot; because it closed in 1803, it is already gone at the 1811 start (`post_closures`, "Grand Portage 1803") and is never raised in play, though it keeps its generic layout, an Ojibwa family camp and a three-man cast (Mr. James Leith and two voyageurs, Joseph Boucher and Michel Lafleur) in the scene. The **portage** is a permanent landmark: a board marked "Grand Portage / Portage" with a beached canoe and a cache at the landing, a red cross on the map's brigade layer (shown at zoom 1.6 or more).

The landing is trodden bare: the bush is cleared within 700 px (about 17 km on the map) of every portage landmark.

## Distances

Grand Portage to Fort William is about 57 km on the map: the NWC's move of 1803 was a short one.
