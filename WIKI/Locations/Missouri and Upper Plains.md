## Description

The Missouri is a trade country rather than a post country. The **Mandan, Hidatsa and Arikara** were farming peoples living in dome-shaped earth lodges behind ditch and palisade, growing corn, beans and squash; the nomadic peoples came with meat, hides and European goods and left with corn, and traders came up from the Assiniboine posts for the same thing. The game paints them as **earth-lodge villages** (earth lodges, garden plots, corn scaffold, cache pit, corn mortar, village palisade, a bullboat) with a seasonal garden harvest and a summer fair, not as tipi camps (see [[Gathering and Gardens]]).

The posts in the list are American. All but Fort Manuel Lisa are dated after 1821 (Fort Union 1828, Fort Clark 1831, Fort Piegan 1831, Fort McKenzie 1832, Fort Berthold 1845, Fort Benton 1846) and so stay out of a 1811 to 1821 game; none has a layout (map points only). The nuisance faction in the window is the [[Americans]] (Astor and Manuel Lisa; design doc §7.3).

## Peoples and camps

Camps in this region: Mandan, Hidatsa, Arikara, Crow, Shoshone, Cheyenne, Dakota (Santee), Lakota (Teton) bands, with the Blood and Peigan (Blackfoot Confederacy) on the northern margin. See [[Country Camps]] for the full list.

## Wildlife

Buffalo country (about 7 buffalo plains, the most of any region), with the Mandan, Crow, Lakota and Cheyenne bands each beside a herd. Hares and caribou round the margins.

## Tips

- Visit a Missouri town in autumn for corn and in winter for the harvested garden plots.
- Dev: `--spawn=Mandan` jumps straight there.
