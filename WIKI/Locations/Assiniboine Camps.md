## Seasonal round

| Date | Move |
|---|---|
| 1 July | They come down the river road and pitch tipis on the trail below York for the trading season |
| 1 September | They go back up-country for the buffalo |
| Winter | Upland near Oxford House, filling their cache with **pemmican** from the buffalo country (14 a week) |

## Trade

Starting cache: 40 pemmican, 6 caribou hides, 20 firewood, 4 beaver pelts, 3 wolf pelts, 1 kettle, 10 water and 6 trade goods. The Assiniboine make pemmican to sell: a camp that sits on more than its winter's worth (30 pieces, or 3 a mouth) takes up to 20 pieces to whichever house will have it; the Company's `BuyProvisions` pays one piece of goods for two [[Pemmican]], only while the post's mess is under 60 days of food. If neither company will deal, the country hears they have pemmican to spare and nobody to sell it to. Their standing with the Company starts at 40 and with the Nor'Westers at 45.

## Relations

Standing with the Cree band starts at 65 ("allies of old"). Hunters of the two nations meeting on the same ground trade hard words (-2 each), it mends 0.3 a day, and under 25 the bands are **at odds** and hunters treat each other as enemies in the bush. Carry tobacco between the camps ("Speak for peace with...", rapport 15, 3 goods, +15). See [[Councils and Accords]] and [[Assiniboine Path]].
