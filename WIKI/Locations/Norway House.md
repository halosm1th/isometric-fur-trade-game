## What is there

The master Mr. Charles Davey and two labourers, Thomas Taylor and Robert Gibson. Starting stock is the standard post stock (see [[Trading Post Ledger]]).

## Why it matters

`docs/world-content-needed.md` lists Norway House as the third priority for a full layout: HBC's inland depot from 1814 and the hub of the York boat brigades. The historical campaign sends news *from* Norway House to the Factor at York about the colony being dispersed (Jun 1815, 40 days) and about Seven Oaks (Jun 1816, 35 days): the place is the Factor's window on the Red River troubles. The "Jack River House" that preceded it (c. 1801, to be checked) is not on the map.

Hill's Gates and Robinson Portage lie above Oxford House on the road between Oxford House and Norway House, not on the York-Oxford road (see [[Routes and Portages]]).
