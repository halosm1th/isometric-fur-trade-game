# Moose Factory

**Status:** Partial. A planned post (custom plan, ten-person cast, own ledger). It is the Southern Department's headquarters on paper (design doc §6.2) but the department's own mechanics (a third Bay depot, its own standing review) are only proposed.

## People

Mr. George Cameron ([[Postmaster]], the master), Joseph Ballenden (clerk), six labourers (Adam Rendall, David Bichan, Alexander Garrick, Hugh Moar, Magnus Halcro, Magnus Inkster) and two women (Nancy Mowat, Mary Cromartie). These are invented cast.

## Role in the world

The design doc (§6.2) makes the Southern Department its own region: Moose Factory, Albany, the East Main coast and the Cree, Ojibwe and Algonquin bands of the James Bay country, with Sault Ste. Marie as its southern anchor. Moose Factory is proposed as the third Bay depot in the HBC message system; in the current build it is an ordinary post with a ledger ([[Trading Post Ledger]]): starting stock 25 pemmican, 20 dried meat, 25 firewood, 40 trade goods, 8 tobacco, 10 water, an axe, 2 fishing lines and 4 beaver pelts.
