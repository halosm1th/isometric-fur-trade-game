## People

Mr. William Robertson (master), Charles Tait, George Spence, Donald Kirkness, Alexander Clouston (labourers) and Isabella Sinclair (woman). Invented cast.

## Role

Fourth in the playable-layout priority list: the Saskatchewan gateway. The Saskatchewan brigade route begins near here and runs to Edmonton House (see [[Routes and Portages]]); Grand Rapids, where the Saskatchewan enters Lake Winnipeg, is the landmark just downstream.
